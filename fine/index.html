Fine.

A gold-buying app for first-time buyers who want to own a little gold and see the proof. The name says how it should feel; the bars in the letters say what you hold. Slogan: “You’re fine. Here’s why.”

Finding the F

Six geometric F marks and three palettes came first. The idea that stayed was separate bars held together.

The Fine F

Luperca’s F, rebuilt with a stem 50% heavier and two gold bars docked on it. In two seconds it reads as an F. In three, as gold bars on a vault post.

Bars in every letter

Every serif, arm and the full stop is a gold bar with a fine carved line. The primary logo shows only the F bars and the stop in gold. The other bars appear in motion.

The brand family

Wordmark, slogan, colour and type in one sheet.

Lit gold

Gold is treated as a material, so it gets light instead of a flat yellow: one narrow highlight, and green reflected in the darker faces. On the phone the highlight follows the tilt, off by default. In print the bars are hot-foil stamped.
