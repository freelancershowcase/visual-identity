Fold Supply

A brand for folding desk accessories, built on the moment work turns back into home. At six the stand folds flat, the mat rolls up and the desk becomes a table again.

The mark

Silver is the stand, felt is the flap. It sits on the baseline like a full stop, so every brand line ends with it. Four lockups share the one stop.

Colour and type

Each colour comes from a material in the range: felt, silver, kraft. Lamp amber is the one colour that isn’t an object, and it only sits on graphite (8.1 : 1).

Icons, lines and motion

Twelve icons on a 48 px grid, each product drawn at work and put away. The fold is the brand’s only motion, 240 ms.

In use

The box seal is the logo, so closing the box makes the mark. One amber stop hides in every tissue sheet.
