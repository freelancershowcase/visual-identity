Notch

A denim trouser label for people who already own a pair they love. You measure it, and every Notch cut is drawn against it. Small drops printed by hand add colour.

Cut, not drawn

Custom die-cut letters: the o is a hook-hole punch, and n, c and h are notcher slots. On the hangtag, slots in the bottom edge give the length: one for Short, two for Regular, three for Long.

Colour and measurement

Raw denim, chalk and four print inks. The inks decorate the drops and never carry status. The measurement graphic lays your pair over ours, to scale.

Pattern bundle

A brochure of trouser-shaped cards at 1 : 5, hung on a ring. The front shows the pair; the back shows its pattern on manila card with real measurements.

Hem index catalogue

An A5 catalogue cut to three heights, so the bottom edges step like hems. The back cover is stamped and numbered by hand.

Directions not taken

Round one, then the two logo routes that lost to Cut, not drawn.
